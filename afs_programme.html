Pre-event 10 May 2026 — Nairobi, Kenya

CSOs Pavilion

Innovation and Social Creation Pavilion

The Innovation and Social Creation Pavilion takes place on 10 May 2026 in Nairobi, ahead of the Africa Forward Summit.

The Pavilion is at once:

  • A multi-stakeholder event supported by around fifty pan-African and French civil society organisations.
  • A space for dialogue and for sharing experiences and solutions.
  • An opportunity to showcase grassroots initiatives that are paving the way for deep transformations in institutions, communities and forms of citizenship.
  • A platform to highlight new ideas and examples of social innovation that contribute to community security, the rule of law and a vibrant civic and cultural life across the continent.
  • A way to support the energy and aspirations of youth and women, while helping to build new shared narratives and to address Africa’s major challenges today.

Africa Forward Summit
